## Horizontal logo v1 — new generation

Use case: logo-brand.
Create an original, refined, playful consumer-social-app logo for the brand "Mootz", spelled exactly M-o-o-t-z. This app lets friends capture real-life moments together and leave meeting dots on a social map. It should feel fun, friendly, tactile, memorable and confidently simple, suitable for a young adult audience. Existing brand direction is deep purple and lilac.
Asset: one clean finished horizontal brand lockup on a genuinely transparent background, not a presentation board or mockup. Wide landscape canvas. Symbol on the left, wordmark on the right with generous optical separation. Center the lockup with balanced margins and plenty of negative space.
Symbol concept: a bold compact abstract lowercase-m / uppercase-M hybrid constructed from two rounded arch-like buddies leaning toward and joining one another, suggesting two people meeting. One unified strong silhouette, soft blunt terminals and clean rounded negative-space cutouts, a slight playful asymmetric lift, no thin detail. It must remain recognizable as an independent app icon. Favor beautifully drawn simple custom geometry, not generic stock interlocking circles, hearts, infinity loops, or map pins. No cartoon faces or limbs needed.
Wordmark: exact text "Mootz" with capital M and lowercase ootz, custom chunky soft rounded sans lettering, careful optical kerning, distinctive friendly forms. Keep the two o letters clearly readable as letters; a gentle paired rhythm can suggest friends. The z adds a small cheeky character without sacrificing readability.
Palette: flat solid deep saturated purple #6D28D9 for the mark and wordmark; if a second color is essential use lilac #C4B5FD very sparingly, but prioritize a one-color reproducible core mark. Sharp clean vector-like edges and accurate spelling.
Constraints: logo only. No tagline, no explanatory labels, no additional text, no borders, no decorative background, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D rendering, no metallic/glass effects, no coin, no chain links, no blockchain logos, no QR, no camera pictogram. Transparent canvas with actual alpha, no painted checkerboard. Deliver a strong considered brand identity rather than a generic AI-startup mark.
